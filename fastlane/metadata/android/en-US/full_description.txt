GAMA Core is a bare-bones Vulkan / OpenGL renderer switcher for low-end devices and minimalists. No animations, no blur, no themes.

• Switch between Vulkan and OpenGL, with a check that the change really applied.
• Works with root or Shizuku.
• Re-applies your Vulkan choice after a reboot.
• Quick Settings tile, home-screen widget and Tasker support.
• Settings backup and restore.

Vulkan does not work equally well on every device. If something misbehaves, switch back to OpenGL or restart the device.
